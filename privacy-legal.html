Privacy Policy

Data we process

  • Account information: username, email address, date of birth for age eligibility, and a one-way password hash.
  • Activity information: favorites, coupons, predictions, virtual Coin/XP activity, chat messages, reports and moderation records.
  • Security and technical information: login time, device/browser information, time zone and a one-way hashed IP identifier. Login audit records are retained for up to 90 days.
  • Model records: pre-match predictions and final results used to measure model performance. Public community percentages are aggregated.

Approximate location

SoccerEdge Pro does not request or access your device's GPS location and does not use the browser geolocation permission. We may derive an approximate country and city/region from network information such as your IP address for localization, security, fraud/abuse prevention, analytics and, where legally permitted, advertising measurement or regional advertising. This is approximate network-based location and is not precise GPS location.

Why we process data

To provide accounts and requested features, secure the service, prevent abuse, moderate community content, calculate virtual game results, improve model quality and comply with legal obligations.

Storage and sharing

Data may be processed by infrastructure, database and email providers strictly to operate the service. Data may be processed outside your country where those services are hosted. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection where applicable. Account and data requests can be submitted through in-app Support. Some records may be retained where required for security or legal compliance.

Local storage

The website uses browser local storage for login sessions, language and interface preferences. Essential storage is required for signed-in features.

Yaklaşık konum

SoccerEdge Pro cihazınızın GPS konumunu istemez veya erişmez ve tarayıcının konum iznini kullanmaz. Yerelleştirme, güvenlik, dolandırıcılık/kötüye kullanımın önlenmesi, analitik ve hukuken izin verilen durumlarda reklam ölçümü veya bölgesel reklam gösterimi amacıyla IP adresi gibi ağ bilgilerinden yaklaşık ülke ve şehir/il-bölge bilgisi türetilebilir. Bu bilgi yaklaşık ağ tabanlı konumdur; kesin GPS konumu değildir.
